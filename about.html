hi, i'm wyatt. creative director and social media manager, based in new york.

what i do.

I work with artists, brands, and influencers to shape their creative direction and social presence, from content, campaigns and brand partnerships to tone, rollout and identity.

my approach.

I start with context and feel to understand where an artist or brand actually exists online and who they’re speaking to. From there, I craft ideas that feel organic, true, and tasteful, not forced or overproduced.

In a digital landscape now full of noise, authenticity is a foundation to establish genuine connection. Turning static strategies into dynamic visual assets across social media demands attention. I live by a simple rule: aspire to inspire.

what i offer.

  • creative direction Shaping the feel and visual direction of artists, brands, or creators across different platforms and mediums.
  • social strategy Platform-native strategy built around taste, timing, and audience behavior.
  • content oversight Directing what gets made, how it looks, and how/where it lives online. Procuring brand partnerships that feel organic.
  • messaging Communicating with your fans in a raw and authentic way that allows them to feel uniquely connected to you.
  • visual / social audit Refining existing assets to ensure maximum impact and clarity.
  • content creation Production of media optimized for social platforms.
  • fan relationship building Converting casual fans into diehard supporters across various platforms and mediums.
  • product design (ui/ux) Building landing pages and websites that are intuitive and optimized for fans’ digital experiences.
  • end-to-end manufacturing Creating custom apparel and physical items through a global network to strengthen brand identity.
